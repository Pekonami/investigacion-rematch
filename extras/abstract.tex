\begin{abstract}
Regular document spanners extract spans from a document and return all
mappings of a query under the all-match semantics. Engines such as
REmatch evaluate such queries through variable-set automata (VAs),
whose determinisation can be exponential. The size of the deterministic
automaton depends on the nondeterministic automaton from which the
construction starts. We study this dependency for two classical
constructions, Glushkov and Thompson, adapted to automata with capture
variables.

We formalise the chain VA $\to$ eVA $\to$ logical-VA, define a Glushkov
construction with capture variables, and prove that the resulting VA
denotes exactly the same document spanner as the source regex formula.
The proof separates the two-layer semantics of regex formulas from the
word semantics of their marker translation, and relates the latter to
the valid runs of the Glushkov VA. It therefore provides the semantic
guarantees needed to compare the number of reachable states obtained by
determinising the Glushkov and Thompson constructions for the same
query, before minimisation, and to identify the conditions under which
one construction is preferable.
\end{abstract}
